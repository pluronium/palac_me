About Me

Bridging Strategy Consulting, 0-to-1 Product Execution & Applied Data Science.

Based in Philadelphia, PA, I've spent the past decade helping organizations turn ambiguous market opportunities into repeatable, measurable growth engines—whether advising Fortune 500 executives, scaling early-stage ventures 10x, or building net-new software and research products from scratch.

My Career Story

My career started on the operational frontlines at Aramark inside the University of Pennsylvania's Chemistry Facilities, where I managed 25 unionized employees across 6 locations, restructured under-performing sites, and earned a Top 10% national "Fast Tracked" leadership designation before supporting the CMO and SVP of Strategy.

From there, I stepped into the hyper-growth startup world as Chief of Staff to the CEO at Nerd Street (NSG). In a single year, we scaled national deployment IT infrastructure, white-label licensing, and turnkey event teams—growing the company from $200K in seed funding ($2M valuation) to a $20M valuation.

Over seven transformative years at InnerView Group (2019–2026), I wore three progressive hats: scaling internal operations during a 200%+ headcount growth phase as Business Operations Manager, advising 20+ Fortune 500 and mid-market enterprises as Managing Consultant, and serving as Product Manager—where I conceived and launched a proprietary research and scoring product that generated over $3M in new revenue and lifted top-line company revenue by 15%.

In June 2026, I transitioned to Adaptive USA as a Manager, leading enterprise client engagements and strategic growth initiatives across key accounts, while completing my Master of Applied Science in Data Science at the Illinois Institute of Technology (Dec 2026).
